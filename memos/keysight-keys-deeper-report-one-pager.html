What it means

  • The mechanism is standards churn, not capex dollars: test demand is leveraged to the rate at which networking standards and speeds turn over — 800G to 1.6T to 3.2T, 112G to 224G SerDes, and the multi-vendor UEC / UALink / PCIe / CXL stack — each transition re-opening a validation cycle.
  • The Spirent twist: the data-center-Ethernet test line was divested to Viavi for $399M, so Keysight's AI-networking test strength is organic (the KAI platform and the former Ixia line) — and the niche is now genuinely contested.
  • AI exposure concentrates in CSG Commercial Communications — ~46% of FY2025 revenue — where the 10-K equates 'wireline' with the data-center ecosystem.
  • Fairly-to-fully valued: comps in line, the Street sees ~6% upside, and a conservative 5-year DCF lands well below spot — the asymmetry skews down if the +56% order surge proves cyclical.
