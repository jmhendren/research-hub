Japan's three listed 'electric-wire' majors are the cleanest public proxy for one secular driver — optical interconnect inside AI datacenters — but they differ enormously in purity, valuation, and risk. For exposure you can't pin to a single winner, an equal-weight basket is the disciplined default: full theme beta, diversified single-name risk. Concentrate in Fujikura for maximum purity and quality; own Sumitomo as the cheap, defensive way to keep a toe in; handle Furukawa with care — it trades nearly as expensive as Fujikura on half the AI purity. The one risk no construction diversifies is the theme itself cooling.

What it means

  • One theme, three concentrations. AI-purity by group operating profit: Fujikura ~81%, Furukawa ~42%, Sumitomo ~18%. Buying Sumitomo is mostly buying automotive wiring harnesses with an AI call option stapled on.
  • Valuation only partly inverts purity. On a consistent EV/EBITDA basis Fujikura trades ~34x, Furukawa ~32x, Sumitomo ~16x — so Furukawa is nearly as expensive as Fujikura on roughly half the purity.
  • The basket diversifies single-name risk, not theme risk. The three multi-bagged together and fell ~7% together in the May–June 2026 rout; a 30% multiple reset takes all three down ~30%.
  • How to choose: equal-weight basket as the disciplined default; Fujikura for purity/quality (peak-multiple risk); Sumitomo for cheap, defensive exposure; Furukawa is the geared middle — most leverage, most beta, least valuation cushion.
