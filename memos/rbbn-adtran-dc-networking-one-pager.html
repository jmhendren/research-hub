What it means

  • Sub-scale, both confirmed three ways: neither RBBN nor ADTN appears in any optical-systems leader tier (Huawei/Ciena/Nokia/ZTE hold >80% of transport systems revenue), each optical franchise runs ~$0.33–$0.38B versus Ciena's ~$5.6B, and sell-side interconnect coverage names neither.
  • AI-DC exposure is real but a minority of each name and more product-positioning than booked revenue: ADTN carries the larger, better-grounded exposure (enterprise+cloud 21% of FY2025 revenue, Q4 optical +33% YoY attributed to cloud DC capacity), while RBBN discloses no data-center revenue line at all and named zero hyperscaler design wins in FY2025.
  • ADTN is the stronger relative call — faster optical growth and a turnaround (FY2025 revenue +17.5%, non-GAAP operating margin to +4.8%, free cash flow +58%) — but carries the discrete ~€304.4M/$351.7M DPLTA cash claim; RBBN is cheaper but more levered, with a May-2026 covenant waiver.
  • A re-rating toward the leader's multiple requires disclosed AI-DC revenue the filings do not yet show; the valuation gap is the opportunity, but the exposure reality only partly supports closing it. Neither is a pure-play AI-interconnect bet.
