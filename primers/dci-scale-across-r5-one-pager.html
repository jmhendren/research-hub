What it means

  • Power, not networking preference, now caps a single data center — and every US frontier operator (Microsoft, Meta, Google, AWS, OpenAI/Stargate) already runs or is building multi-data-center AI clusters in production, so demand is grid-physics-structural rather than a refresh cycle.
  • Conviction is layer-shaped: L4 DWDM line systems ($8.2B, 47% of the 2029 base) and L1 coherent pluggables ($4.3B) carry HIGH-confidence triangulation; L2/L3/L5 are derived attribution shares — about 29% of the headline rides on them.
