2. Capex by Archetype, 2025–2030

Each year's pool is split across the five archetypes per the Base mix scenario. Inference is the dominant archetype across the period (38–52% share). Agentic ramps from 2% in 2025 to 30% by 2030 — the structural shift the model is built to size.

TrainingCore Inference Agentic Legacy Edge
Inference (burgundy) is the dominant archetype 2026–2030, but Agentic (ochre) is the fastest grower — from $9B in 2025 to $558B by 2030, a 36% share of the 2030 pool. TrainingCore peaks in absolute dollars in 2030 ($326B) but loses share (28% → 18%) as the build matures.

5. BOM Intensity by Archetype

Each archetype allocates the same 15 BOM lines differently. The contrasts are load-bearing: TrainingCore is GPU-heavy (48%) with no custom ASIC; Inference shifts 7% of compute to custom ASIC (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA) and lifts memory share to 13%; Agentic doubles memory share to 21% (KV-cache scales with context length and concurrent agents); Legacy is CPU-dominant (11%) with the highest LSE share (16%); Edge carries the highest power-infrastructure share (17% across primary + UPS + PDU) because modular edge DCs front-load power kit per MW.
