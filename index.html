NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) — Historically Cheap on a Relative Basis?

An answer-shape brief on one question: is NVDA actually cheap right now? Yes on the evidence — ≈31x trailing earnings is about half its five-year average (~63x), the next-twelve-month multiple is the lowest since June 2019, and NVDA is the cheapest of the compared AI-semi set (AMD, Broadcom, TSMC) on trailing earnings outright and growth-adjusted (forward PEG ≈0.76 vs 0.92–2.15; BofA frames 0.28 on 2027 estimates). Two honest caveats: the discount fades over a 20-year window, and the low multiple prices consensus estimates that assume revenue nearly doubles again — the market charging for estimate risk, not a free discount. 57 citations, 42 primary snapshots, dual-family audited.
